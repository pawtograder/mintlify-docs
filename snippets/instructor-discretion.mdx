<Note>
**What your instructor configured wins.** This page describes how Pawtograder behaves in the setup most courses use. Almost every rule below is a per-assignment or per-course setting, and your instructor chose the settings for your course on purpose, as part of how they want the course to run. If your assignment behaves differently from what you read here, that is the configuration working as intended, not a bug and not something to argue about. Your assignment page and your course's own policies are the authority on what applies to you — ask your course staff if you are not sure.
</Note>
