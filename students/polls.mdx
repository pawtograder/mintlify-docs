# Polls

Polls are a quick and easy way to answer questions during class. Your instructor may use polls to check understanding, gather feedback, or spark discussion.

<Note>
The poll link and QR code belong to the course as a whole rather than to a single poll, so they always open whichever poll your instructor opened most recently. If more than one poll is open at the same time, the link may not open the one you expect — ask your instructor which poll they want you to answer. This is a known issue with an open report against Pawtograder, and a fix is on the way.
</Note>

## Answering Polls

There are two ways to reach the current poll. Both land on the same page.

### Via QR Code or Link

Your instructor's poll screen shows **Answer at:** followed by a URL, along with a QR code for that same URL:

1. **Scan the QR code** with your phone camera, or open the URL your instructor displays
2. The poll question and answer options appear
3. Select your answer(s) from the options displayed
4. Click **Complete** to send your response

If the poll does not require login, you can respond immediately without signing in.

### Via the Polls Page

You can also access polls from inside Pawtograder:

1. Open your course and choose **Polls** from the course menu — it appears only if your instructor has enabled polls for the course
2. The **Live Polls** page lists every poll that is currently open, one row per poll
3. Click **Answer Poll**. The poll opens in a new browser tab
4. Select your answer(s) and submit

## Login Requirements

Polls can be configured by your instructor to require login or allow anonymous responses:

| Mode | How It Works |
|------|--------------|
| **Login not required** | Scan the QR code or use the link to answer immediately without signing in. Your response is recorded with no profile attached. |
| **Login required** | You must sign in to Pawtograder before answering. Your response is associated with your course profile, and you can only answer once. |

<Note>
If a poll requires login and you're not signed in, you'll see a **Login Required** screen with a **Sign In** button that returns you to the poll after you authenticate.
</Note>

## Poll Types

Your instructor builds the question, so what you see depends on what they authored. The poll builder offers two types:

### Single Choice

- Select **exactly one answer** from the options
- Uses radio button selection
- Clicking a new option automatically deselects your previous choice

### Multiple Choice

- Select **one or more answers** from the options
- Uses checkbox selection
- Choose all options that apply to the question

An instructor can also hand-write the question definition directly, so you may occasionally see a question type that isn't a simple list of choices.

<Note>
Answering is required by default. If you leave a question blank, the poll will not submit until you pick something.
</Note>

## Submitting Your Response

1. Review your selected answer(s)
2. Click **Complete**
3. A **Thank You!** screen confirms that your response was submitted

Whether you can answer again depends on the poll's login setting:

- **Login required**: your first response is final. A second attempt shows "You have already submitted a response. Polls with login require you to submit only once."
- **Login not required**: the confirmation screen invites you to refresh the page to answer again

## Poll Availability

- Polls are only available while your instructor has them open — they use **Start Poll** and **Stop Poll** on their poll screen
- Once your instructor closes a poll, it disappears: it drops off the **Live Polls** list, and the poll page reports that no poll is available

## Troubleshooting

### "No Live Polls Available"

- Your instructor doesn't have any polls open right now
- Wait for your instructor to start a new poll
- The **Live Polls** page updates on its own when a poll opens, so you don't need to reload it

### "No Live Poll Available"

- The poll tab does not refresh itself. Reload it to pick up the poll your instructor just opened
- If reloading doesn't help, your instructor hasn't started a poll yet

### QR code not scanning

- Make sure your camera is focused on the QR code
- Try moving closer or adjusting the angle
- Use the URL shown next to **Answer at:** on your instructor's screen instead

### Login prompt appears

- The poll requires authentication
- Sign in with your Pawtograder credentials
- After signing in, you'll be redirected back to the poll

## Tips for Students

- **Have your device ready**: When your instructor announces a poll, be ready to scan the QR code quickly
- **Read carefully**: Take a moment to read the question and all options before answering
- **Don't overthink**: Polls are usually meant for quick responses, not extended deliberation
- **Ask questions**: If you don't understand the poll question, ask your instructor for clarification
