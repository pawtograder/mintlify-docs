# Cloud Workspace

Some courses offer a **cloud workspace** — a browser-based VS Code editor that runs on the course's servers with the same Java, Python, Node, and other tools the autograder uses. When it is available for an assignment, an **Open in Coder** card appears on your assignment page.

<Note>
Cloud workspaces are only available when your instructor has enabled the **cloud-workspaces** feature for the course. If you do not see the card, your course is not using this feature.
</Note>

## Opening your workspace

1. Go to your assignment page.
2. Find the **Open in Coder** card below the assignment description.
3. Click **Open workspace**. Pawtograder creates your workspace and clones your assignment repository into it.

The card walks through three startup steps:

| Step | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Build** | The workspace container is starting. |
| **Clone** | Your assignment repository is being cloned into the workspace. |
| **Editor** | VS Code (code-server) is starting up. |

Once all three steps are complete, the card shows an **Open in VS Code** link. Click it to open the editor in a new tab.

## Linking your Forgejo account

The workspace clones your repository using your Forgejo account. If you have not linked Forgejo to Coder yet, the card shows a **Link Forgejo account** button. Click it to go through the one-time authorization flow, then return to the assignment page. The card re-checks your link status when you come back.

## Workspace lifecycle

- **Automatic stop** — Your workspace stops automatically after one hour of inactivity (no open browser tab connected to it). Your files on the home volume are preserved.
- **Restart** — If your workspace stopped or encountered an error, click **Open workspace** again. The card restarts it and your files are kept.
- **Restarting state** — If the workspace is in the middle of restarting, the card shows **Restarting**. Wait a moment and it will update automatically.

## Pushing your work

The workspace is pre-configured to push to your assignment repository using your Forgejo credentials. Use the VS Code Source Control panel or the terminal (`git push`) as you normally would. Pushes trigger the autograder the same way a local push does.

## Troubleshooting

| What you see | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| **Clone failed** | The card shows the reason. Check that your Forgejo account is linked (see above) and try again. |
| **Account conflict** | Your Coder account was created with a different email address. Contact your course staff. |
| **Open workspace does nothing** | You may be viewing the assignment as a staff member. Cloud workspaces are only available to enrolled students. |

If the workspace keeps failing to start, contact your course staff and describe which step failed and any error message shown.
