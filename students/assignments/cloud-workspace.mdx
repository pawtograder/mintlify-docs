# Opening a Cloud Workspace

Some courses provide a cloud workspace for each assignment: a VS Code environment in the browser, pre-configured with the same tools the autograder uses, with your assignment repository already cloned. If your course has this enabled, you will see an **Open in Coder** card on the assignment page.

## Before you start

You need a Forgejo account linked in Coder before a workspace can be created. If the link is missing, the card shows a **Link Forgejo account** prompt. Click it to open Coder's Forgejo authorization page, approve the connection, and return to the assignment page. The card re-checks automatically when you come back to the tab.

## Starting your workspace

Click **Open workspace** on the assignment page. The card walks through three startup steps:

| Step | What is happening |
| --- | --- |
| **Build** | Coder is scheduling and starting the workspace pod. |
| **Clone** | Your assignment repository is being cloned into the workspace. |
| **Editor** | VS Code (code-server) is starting up. |

Each step shows a spinner while it is running and a checkmark when it finishes. If a step fails, the card names the step and shows a **Retry** button.

Once all three steps are complete, an **Open in VS Code** link appears. Click it to open the editor in a new tab.

## Returning to your workspace

Your workspace stops automatically after an hour of inactivity. When you come back to the assignment page and click **Open workspace** again, the card restarts it and walks through the same steps. Your files are preserved on the workspace's home volume — the clone step is skipped if your repository is already there.

## Troubleshooting

**The card shows "Link Forgejo account"** — Your Forgejo account is not linked in Coder yet. Click the prompt, approve the connection, and return to the assignment page.

**A step fails with an error message** — Click **Retry**. If the clone step fails, the message says why (for example, a missing Forgejo link or a network timeout). Fix the underlying issue and retry.

**The card shows "Contact course staff"** — Your Coder account was created with an old email address and cannot be signed in to. Ask your instructor or a TA to reset it.

**The card shows "Restarting"** — The workspace is being restarted after a failed start. The card polls automatically and will update when the restart finishes.

<Note>
Staff viewing the course as a student do not see the Open in Coder card. The workspace would be created under the staff member's own account, not the student's.
</Note>
