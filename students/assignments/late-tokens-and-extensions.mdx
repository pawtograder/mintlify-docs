# Late Tokens and Extensions

There are two ways your assignment deadline can move past its original date:

- **Late tokens**: you choose to spend a token to extend a deadline.
- **Instructor-granted extensions**: staff manually add time to your deadline, separately from tokens.

## Late Tokens

Pawtograder supports late submission policies based on "late tokens." Your instructor decides how many late tokens each student in the course gets, and caps how many of them can be spent on any one assignment. Each token extends your deadline by 24 hours, and the extended deadline is then treated as your deadline. Pawtograder applies no automatic late penalty on top.

Whether you have to spend a token *before* the deadline depends on the assignment:

- **Tokens must be applied beforehand.** This is the default. The dialog says so: "Tokens are not automatically applied - to use them, you must use this form to apply them BEFORE the due date passes." Once your deadline has passed, the control is replaced by "(Firm date: You have passed the due date)".
- **Tokens are applied automatically when you submit late.** Where your instructor turned that requirement off, the dialog reads "Tokens can be applied before the due date using this form, or will be automatically applied when you submit after it", and after the deadline the assignment page reads "(Deadline passed: submitting will auto-apply a late token if you have one remaining)". A late push spends however many whole days of tokens it takes to cover how late it is, so a push 30 hours late costs two tokens. If your balance can't cover it, the push is rejected and no token is spent.

<Warning>
Spending a token is immediate and irreversible: "Once you consume a late token, it is consumed immediately. You will not be able to undo this action."
</Warning>

### Applying Late Tokens

On each assignment page where late tokens can be applied, you'll see an **Extend Due Date** button next to your due date. If your course uses suggested due dates, that button reads **Extend Resubmission Deadline** instead: a token can only ever move the hard deadline you resubmit until, never the suggested date shown as **Due**.

### If You Don't See the Button

Where you can't extend a deadline, the button is replaced by a short message saying why:

- **(No late submissions allowed)**: your course grants no late tokens at all.
- **(You have no remaining late tokens)**: you have spent your whole course allowance.
- **(You may not extend the due date for this assignment any further)**: you have reached your instructor's per-assignment cap.
- **(Firm date: You have passed the due date)**: the deadline has passed on an assignment whose tokens had to be applied beforehand.
- **(You may not extend the due date for this assignment as you finalized early)**: see below.

### Finalizing a Submission Early

On an assignment with a self-review, your instructor may offer a **Finalize Submission Early** button, which moves your deadline to now so that the self-review opens immediately. Confirming it warns you that "You will not be able to change your submission after this." See [Completing a Self-Review](/students/assignments/self-review).

<Warning>
Finalizing early permanently gives up your ability to extend that assignment. The late-token control turns into "(You may not extend the due date for this assignment as you finalized early)", and a later push cannot spend tokens automatically either. Pushing again after finalizing tells you that "you have already finalized your work for this assignment, so late tokens were not applied".
</Warning>

### Gifted Late Tokens

Instructors can gift additional late tokens to individual students beyond the class-wide allocation. Your balance is your course allowance, less the tokens you have spent, plus any your instructor has granted you. So gifted tokens:

- Are added to your available token balance automatically
- Work exactly like your original tokens — each extends a deadline by 24 hours
- Are included in the "You have *N* late tokens remaining" count in the dialog
- Can be used even after you've exhausted your initial allocation

For example, if you have spent your whole allowance and your instructor gifts you one more token, you have one left to spend.

### Late Tokens and Group Assignments

For group assignments, late token semantics are designed to be straightforward and fair:

- Any group member with at least one token available can apply a late token (so long as the assignment's per-assignment token cap hasn't been reached). It does not matter if a groupmate is out of tokens.
- All group members have a token deducted from their balance.
- All group members see the same extended due date.

## Instructor-Granted Extensions

Separately from late tokens, instructors can grant extensions directly. These are different from late tokens in two important ways:

- **They don't consume your token budget.** Your remaining token count is unchanged.
- **You don't apply them yourself.** The extension is added by staff and takes effect automatically; your due date simply moves.

Extensions can be granted:

- **For a specific assignment**, just for you (or your group)
- **Across all of your assignments**, e.g. as a blanket accommodation

A course-wide extension is written onto each of your assignments separately, including assignments your instructor creates later. Whether it also covers assignments whose deadline is tied to your lab section is your instructor's choice. Changing the course-wide extension afterwards does not retroactively alter extensions that have already been written onto your assignments.

When an extension is in effect, the assignment page shows your adjusted due date, with the same "(*N*-hour extension applied, …)" note that late tokens produce. You don't need to do anything to "apply" it.
