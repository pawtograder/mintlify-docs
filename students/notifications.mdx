# Notifications

Pawtograder keeps a notification inbox inside the app, emails you about discussion and course activity, and — while you have an open office hours chat — can alert you in your browser.

## Where notifications appear

- **In-app inbox**: the inbox icon in the top-right corner of every course page. A blue badge shows your unread count, capped at `9+`. Click the icon for your recent notifications and a **View all** link; a **Mark all as read** button appears once you have more than one unread.
- **Email**: Pawtograder emails you about discussion posts and replies, office hours activity, regrade request activity, group invitations and membership changes, and being added to a course.
- **Browser, sound, tab title and favicon**: for new messages in an office hours chat, Pawtograder can show a desktop notification, play a sound, flash the browser tab title, and put an unread count on the tab's favicon. All four fire only while you are *not* actively looking at the page, and they follow your active help request across the whole site — not just the office hours pages.

<Note>
Desktop notifications also need your browser's permission. Grant it from **Notification Settings** below, or from the **Enable** prompt Pawtograder shows inside a help request chat. If your browser has already blocked notifications for the site, Pawtograder says so and you have to re-allow them from the lock icon in your browser's address bar.
</Note>

## The notifications page

**View all** in the inbox popover opens the full notifications page. It is not in the course menu, so that link is the only way in. Notifications are grouped into **Regrade Requests**, **Office Hours**, **Discussion Threads**, and **Other**. Each group has its own **Mark all as read** and **Delete all** buttons, alongside a page-wide pair at the top.

<Warning>
**Delete all** permanently removes those notifications and cannot be undone.
</Warning>

## Notification Settings

Click your avatar in the top-right corner to open the user menu, then choose **Notification Settings**.

**Office Hours Chat Notifications** controls the four in-browser channels: **Browser Notifications**, **Sound Notifications** (with a volume slider), **Title Flashing**, and **Favicon Badge**. All are enabled by default, and they save as soon as you flip them. **Test Notification** fires a sample so you can confirm they work.

**Email Notification Preferences** has one setting for students, **Discussion Board Notifications**:

- **Immediate**: an email as soon as a new post or reply arrives
- **Digest**: one summary email per hour instead
- **Disabled**: no discussion emails at all

Click **Save Preferences** to apply your email choice.

## Watching posts and following topics

You are only notified about discussion activity you have opted into, and the two kinds of subscription differ:

- **Watch a post** to be notified of every reply to it. Replying to a post watches it automatically.
- **Follow a topic** to be notified of new posts in that topic — but not of replies to them.

Every discussion email ends with a footer saying which of the two brought you that email, plus one-click links to unfollow that post or that topic, open your notification settings, or disable discussion emails entirely. Opening an unsubscribe link takes effect right away.

## What triggers a notification

- A new message in an office hours help request you are part of
- A staff member is assigned to your help request, or its status changes
- A reply on a discussion post you watch, or a new post in a topic you follow
- Your discussion post is merged into another one as a duplicate
- A new comment on a regrade request you are involved in, or a change to its status, including escalation (see [Regrade Requests](/students/assignments/regrade-requests))
- You are invited to a group, added to one, or removed from one, or a group join request is filed or decided (see [Forming Groups](/students/assignments/form-groups)). Group notifications are held back until the assignment is released, so instructors can set groups up in advance.
- You are added to a course

<Note>
Creating a help request notifies course staff, not you. Nothing notifies you when your position in the office hours queue changes — the floating help widget shows your position live instead.
</Note>

## Troubleshooting

If you stop receiving browser notifications:

1. Confirm **Browser Notifications** is still on in **Notification Settings**, then use **Test Notification**
2. Confirm Pawtograder still has notification permission in your browser's site settings
3. Check your OS-level "Do Not Disturb" or Focus settings
4. Reload the page after granting permission so Pawtograder picks up the change

<Tip>
Test from a different tab or window. Browser, sound, title and favicon alerts deliberately stay silent while the Pawtograder tab is the one you are looking at.
</Tip>
