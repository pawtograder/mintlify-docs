# Office Hours

Pawtograder's office hours, if enabled by your instructor, let you ask course staff for live help. Your request joins a help queue, and any staff member on duty for that queue can pick it up. Requests are worked oldest first, so nothing you do moves you up the queue.

Requests are public by default. Public requests stay visible to your classmates, so someone with the same question can read the answer instead of asking again. You can mark a request private instead, which limits it to course staff and the students named on it.

## Get Help Button

A **Get Help** button sits in the bottom-right corner of course pages. Only students see it, and it is absent in two situations: on the office hours pages themselves, and while you have an open request, when a widget for that request takes its place.

The button carries a short status line. You'll see "Staff ready now!" when the default queue is staffed and nothing is waiting, or "Queue is short" when fewer than five requests are open.

## Help Drawer

Clicking **Get Help** opens a right-side drawer, also titled **Get Help**:

- **About Office Hours in** *your course*: your instructor's office hours description, when they have written one
- **Pinned Discussion Posts**: an expandable list of pinned threads, with a **Browse All Discussion Posts** button
- **Your Active Request**: the status and queue position of your open request, with a **View Request** button
- **Chat with course staff live!**: one card per queue that is accepting requests

Each queue card gives the queue's name and description, how many requests are open, the queue type, and the staff on duty. Below the card you get either the end time of the current shift and who is on next, or "This queue is not currently staffed" with the next scheduled time. Staffed queues offer **New Request** and **View Full Queue**; unstaffed ones offer **View Queue**. A **View Full Schedule** button at the bottom of the drawer opens the office hours page.

Practice queues carry a **DEMO - NOT A REAL QUEUE** badge. Requests on a demo queue do not require anyone to be on duty.

## Browsing Queues and Your Requests

The office hours page has two views, switched by the links at its top left:

- **Browse Queues**, the default view, lists the queues on the left. Pick one to see its description, its **Upcoming Schedule** (the next five sessions, with a session in progress marked 🟢 Now), and its open requests. **View Full Schedule** opens that queue's weekly schedule.
- **My Requests** lists your open requests and then your resolved ones, including group requests you were added to.

When your course has only one queue there is nothing to browse, so Pawtograder sends you straight to it.

## Finding Requests Like Yours

Someone may have asked your question already. The search box in the office hours header matches request text, student names, queue names, and request IDs. It shows up to six matches as you type, and pressing Enter opens the full **Search Help Requests** page, where you can also filter by status and by queue.

You can open a classmate's public request and reply in its chat. You cannot add yourself to it as a participant, and you won't get its notifications unless you press **Watch** on it. The chat becomes read-only once a request is resolved or closed.

## If You Are Banned from Office Hours

Course staff can ban a student from office hours. While a ban is active, every office hours page is replaced by a notice giving the reason, whether the ban is permanent, and the time remaining when it isn't. You cannot submit requests, use office hours chat, or join video calls until it lifts. A warning issued without a ban appears above the page instead, and you keep full access.
