# Office Hours Overview

Pawtograder's office hours are a live help queue. Students file help requests, staff go on duty by starting work on a queue, and a staff member claims each request and answers it in chat, on a video call, or in person until it is resolved.

Everything described here lives under **Manage → Office Hours**, which instructors and graders can open and students cannot. The student-facing side is documented in [Office Hours for Students](/students/office-hours).

## The office hours header

Three items sit at the top of every staff office hours page:

- **Working** is the landing page. Before you go on duty it lists the course's active queues; once you are on duty it lists the requests you are responsible for. A green badge beside it names the queue you are working, or counts them when you are working more than one.
- **All Requests** browses one queue at a time, resolved requests included.
- **Settings** is a menu rather than a page. Its entries are the configuration and reporting surfaces listed below.

A **Search requests...** box sits at the right of the header, next to a red **Stop Working (n)** button that appears only while you are on duty. On a request page, a breadcrumb shows the queue name followed by the request number, and the queue name links back to that queue in All Requests.

| Settings entry | What it does | Documented in |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Queue Management** | Create, edit, and delete queues, and edit the course's office hours description. | [Queue Configuration](/staff/office-hours/queue-configuration) |
| **Assignment Management** | Read-only record of who worked which queue and when, plus force-end and delete. | [Staffing and Time Tracking](/staff/office-hours/staffing-and-time-tracking) |
| **Templates** | Prompt structures students fill in when filing a request. | [Queue Configuration](/staff/office-hours/queue-configuration#templates) |
| **Moderation** | Warnings and bans against individual students. | [Moderation and Karma](/staff/office-hours/moderation-and-karma) |
| **Student Karma** | Private staff notes and a hand-set score per student. | [Moderation and Karma](/staff/office-hours/moderation-and-karma#student-karma) |
| **Student Activity** | Recent office hours activity, moderation, and karma for one student. | [Moderation and Karma](/staff/office-hours/moderation-and-karma#student-activity) |
| **Time Tracking** | Per-TA time on requests, with a CSV export. | [Staffing and Time Tracking](/staff/office-hours/staffing-and-time-tracking#time-tracking) |
| **Feedback** | Thumbs-up and thumbs-down ratings students leave when they resolve. Instructors only. | [Help Requests](/staff/office-hours/help-requests#student-feedback) |

<Note>
Graders see seven of the eight entries. **Feedback** is instructor-only, and its underlying data is readable only by instructors and by the student who submitted it, so a grader has no route to it. Deleting a moderation action or a work session is also restricted to instructors.
</Note>

## Finding requests

### All Requests

Pick a queue from the list on the left, which shows every queue in the course including inactive ones, with its open count. The pane on the right splits that queue into **Open Requests** and **Resolved Requests** with counts, and offers a search box matching request text and student names, a status filter, and an **Oldest First** and **Newest First** toggle.

Open requests are always ordered oldest first, whatever the toggle says; the toggle reorders the resolved list only. Nothing you or a student does moves a request up the open list.

### Search

The header search box shows a handful of matches as you type. Pressing Enter opens the full **Search Help Requests** page, which matches request text, student names, queue names, and request IDs, and adds status chips with counts and a queue filter. Results put in-progress requests first, then open, then resolved and closed, each group newest first. The query, status, and queue are all in the URL, so a search is worth bookmarking or pasting to a colleague.
