# Grader roles and capabilities

Every course has four built-in roles: **instructor**, **grader**, **student**, and
**admin**. Instructors now have a fifth tool: custom grader roles that bundle a
specific set of capabilities, so a head TA can manage assignments or review
assignments without holding full instructor access.

## Built-in roles and their capabilities

| Role | Capabilities |
| --- | --- |
| **instructor** | All four capabilities: `manage_review_assignments`, `publish_groups`, `manage_assignments`, `manage_roles` |
| **grader** | None by default. Can be given additional capabilities through a custom role |
| **student** | No capabilities |
| **admin** | No capabilities (platform administration is separate) |

## Capabilities

| Capability | What it allows |
| --- | --- |
| `manage_review_assignments` | View, create, edit, and delete review assignments for the class. Enables **Bulk Assign**, **Reassign**, and the **All Assignments** table on the Grading Assignments page |
| `publish_groups` | Create, edit, and delete groups; assign mentors; publish groups to students. Without it, **Manage Groups** is read-only |
| `manage_assignments` | Create and edit assignments, configure autograders, sync and create student repositories. Without it, **New Assignment** and **Edit Assignment** are hidden |
| `manage_roles` | Create, rename, and delete custom grader roles; choose their capabilities. Cannot be granted to a custom role |

Instructors hold all four capabilities and are unaffected by this system. Graders
hold none by default; a grader on a custom role holds everything the default
grader role holds plus whatever the custom role adds.

## Managing custom roles

Open **Course Settings → Enrollments** and navigate to the **Roles** tab (visible
to instructors only). From there you can:

- **Create a role** — give it a name and choose its capabilities (any combination
  of `manage_review_assignments`, `publish_groups`, and `manage_assignments`).
  `manage_roles` cannot be added to a custom role.
- **Rename a role** — edit the name inline. Names are unique within the course,
  case-insensitively.
- **Delete a role** — only possible when no one is currently on that role.

<Note>
You can only grant a capability you yourself hold in that class. An instructor
holds all four, so they can grant any of the three grantable capabilities.
</Note>

## Assigning a grader to a custom role

On the **Enrollments** page, find the grader's row and change their **Role**
dropdown from the default grader role to the custom role. Only graders can be
placed on custom roles. Changing someone's app role (e.g. from grader to
instructor) always moves them back to that role's default, so access follows the
role automatically.

## What graders on custom roles see

A grader on a custom role sees the same navigation as any other grader, plus the
controls unlocked by their capabilities:

- **`manage_review_assignments`** — the **Assignment Management** row and
  Edit/Delete icons on Grading Assignments; **Bulk Assign** and **Reassign**
  links; the full **All Assignments** table.
- **`publish_groups`** — all edit controls on **Manage Groups** (create, edit,
  delete groups; assign mentors; publish).
- **`manage_assignments`** — **New Assignment** in the nav; **Edit Assignment**
  in the assignment menu; the **(Edit)** link on Manage Groups.

Without a capability, those controls are hidden and the corresponding pages
redirect back to the assignment list.
