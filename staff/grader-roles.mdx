# Grader roles and capabilities

Every course has four built-in roles — **instructor**, **grader**, **student**, and **admin** — and each person in the course is assigned one. Instructors can now create additional custom grader roles that extend the default grader role with specific capabilities, so a head TA or a specialized grader can do exactly what their role requires without needing full instructor access.

## Built-in capabilities

Each capability unlocks a specific set of actions in the course. The four capabilities are:

| Capability | What it allows |
|---|---|
| `manage_review_assignments` | See and delete review assignments; use Bulk Assign and Reassign |
| `publish_groups` | Create, edit, and publish groups; use all controls on Manage Groups |
| `manage_assignments` | Create and edit assignments; sync and create student repositories |
| `manage_roles` | Create, rename, and delete custom grader roles; choose their capabilities |

By default, only the instructor role holds all four capabilities. The default grader role holds none of them. A custom role can hold any combination of the first three; `manage_roles` is always instructor-only and cannot be added to a custom role.

## What graders see without a capability

When a grader lacks a capability, the controls that require it are hidden rather than shown as disabled:

- **Without `manage_review_assignments`**: The Assignment Management row and row-level Edit/Delete icons are hidden on Grading Assignments. Bulk Assign and Reassign redirect back to Grading Assignments.
- **Without `publish_groups`**: Manage Groups is read-only. Groups, mentors, and CSV export remain; every change control is hidden.
- **Without `manage_assignments`**: New Assignment, the Edit Assignment menu item, and the (Edit) link on Manage Groups are hidden. The `/new` and `/edit` pages redirect to the assignment list.

Instructors see no change.

## Creating a custom grader role

You need the `manage_roles` capability (instructors have it by default).

1. Open **Course Settings → Enrollments** and find the **Roles** section, or navigate to `manage/course/roles`.
2. Click **New Role**.
3. Enter a name. Role names must be non-blank and are unique within the course, ignoring case.
4. Check the capabilities you want the role to hold. You can only grant capabilities you yourself hold in this course.
5. Click **Save**.

The new role immediately appears in the role selector when you edit a grader's enrollment.

<Note>
You can only add capabilities to a custom role, not remove capabilities from it. A custom role always inherits everything the default grader role holds, plus whatever you add. If the default grader role gains a new capability in a future update, every grader — including those on custom roles — gains it automatically.
</Note>

## Assigning a custom role to a grader

1. Open **Course Settings → Enrollments**.
2. Find the grader in the roster.
3. Click the role selector on their row and choose the custom role.

Changing a grader's app role (for example, from grader to student) always moves them to that role's default, discarding any custom role assignment.

<Warning>
`manage_roles` does not let you assign roles to people. Assigning a role to a grader is always instructor-only, controlled by the `user_roles` database policies.
</Warning>

## Deleting a custom role

A custom role can be deleted only when no grader is currently assigned to it. Reassign any graders on the role first, then delete it from the Roles page.

The four default roles (instructor, grader, student, admin) are fixed and cannot be deleted or renamed.
