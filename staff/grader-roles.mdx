# Custom grader roles

Every course starts with four fixed roles: **instructor**, **grader**, **student**, and **admin**. Instructors can create additional grader roles — such as "Head TA" or "Lab Leader" — and choose which capabilities each one carries. A grader on a custom role holds everything the default grader role holds, plus whatever the custom role adds.

## Capabilities

Capabilities are the actions a role can perform. The four available capabilities are:

| Capability | What it allows |
| --- | --- |
| `manage_review_assignments` | See and delete review assignments for the class. Creating and editing review assignments remain instructor-only. |
| `manage_assignments` | Create and edit assignments, sync autograder and handout changes to student repositories, and create student repositories when a save moves the release date into the past. Reading and deleting assignments remain instructor-only. |
| `publish_groups` | Make changes to groups on **Manage Groups** (add/remove members, rename, delete, publish). Without this capability, **Manage Groups** is read-only: groups, mentors, and CSV export are still visible. |
| `manage_roles` | Create, rename, and delete custom grader roles, and choose their capabilities. This capability cannot be added to a custom role — only instructors hold it. |

The default grader role has none of these capabilities. The default instructor role has all four.

## Opening the page

Open **Course Settings → Enrollments**, then select the **Roles** tab. The entry is instructor-only.

## Creating a custom role

1. Click **New Role**.
2. Enter a name. Role names must be non-blank and unique within the course (case-insensitive).
3. Check the capabilities you want the role to carry. You can only grant capabilities you hold yourself.
4. Click **Save**.

The new role appears in the roles list and is immediately available to assign to graders.

## Assigning a role to a grader

Custom roles can only be assigned to graders. Changing someone's app role (for example, promoting a grader to instructor) always moves them to that role's default, overriding any custom role assignment.

To assign a custom role:

1. Open **Course Settings → Enrollments**.
2. Find the grader in the roster.
3. Select the custom role from the **Role** dropdown on their row.

The change takes effect immediately. The grader's access updates to reflect the new role's capabilities.

## Editing and deleting roles

Click the edit icon on a role's row to rename it or change its capabilities. You cannot add a capability you do not hold yourself.

A role that has at least one grader assigned to it cannot be deleted. Reassign or remove those graders first.

The four default roles (instructor, grader, student, admin) are fixed and cannot be renamed, edited, or deleted.

## What graders on custom roles see

A grader's navigation and available actions follow their capabilities:

- **`manage_review_assignments`** — The **Grading Assignments** page shows the Assignment Management row and row-level Edit/Delete icons. **Bulk Assign** and **Reassign** are accessible. Without this capability, those controls are hidden.
- **`manage_assignments`** — **New Assignment** and the **Edit Assignment** menu item are visible. The `/new` and `/edit` pages are accessible. Without this capability, those pages redirect back to the assignment list.
- **`publish_groups`** — **Manage Groups** shows all change controls (add/remove members, rename, delete, publish). Without this capability, the page is read-only.

Instructors see no change regardless of which capabilities are on custom roles.
