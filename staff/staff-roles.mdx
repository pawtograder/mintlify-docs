# Staff Roles

**Course Settings → Staff Roles** is where instructors create custom grader roles, choose what each role can do, and assign graders to those roles. It is instructor-only: a grader who opens the URL sees an explanation and no controls.

The page is a section with a side menu, similar to Manage Assignments. The three pages in the menu are **Create / Edit Role**, **Staff & Roles**, and **Roles & Capabilities**.

## Roles and the default grader role

Every course starts with one built-in role: **Grader**. The Grader role is the default for all graders and cannot be renamed or deleted. Custom roles you create start as a copy of Grader — they inherit whatever capabilities Grader currently holds — and you can then turn capabilities on or off independently.

Instructors always have full access to everything. The Staff Roles page controls only what graders and custom roles can do.

## Creating a custom role

<Steps>
<Step title="Open Staff Roles">
Go to **Course Settings → Staff Roles**. The page opens on **Create / Edit Role**.
</Step>
<Step title="Create the role">
Click **Create a role**. Enter a name (for example, "Head TA") and click **Create role**. The new role starts with everything a Grader can do.
</Step>
<Step title="Edit its capabilities">
Click **Edit a role**, select the new role, then click **Edit capabilities for [role name]**. This takes you to **Roles & Capabilities** with that role selected.
</Step>
</Steps>

Role names must be unique within the course (case-insensitive) and no longer than 60 characters.

## Roles & Capabilities

**Roles & Capabilities** shows every capability for the selected role as an on/off switch. Use the dropdown at the top to switch between roles.

| Manage Assignments | Groups | Manage groups | Set group mentors and copy groups from another assignment under Manage Groups. |

| Manage Assignments | Reviews | Manage review assignments | Create, edit, delete, and clear review assignments under Grading Assignments. |

| Gradebook | Grades | Manage gradebook | View the staff Gradebook, create and arrange columns, import and release grades, and override scores. |
| Office Hours | Help requests | Help students | Take, assign, resolve, and moderate help requests in Office Hours. |

Changes save immediately. The grader's pages update the next time they load a page.

<Note>
The page only shows capabilities that exist in the database. As new capabilities are added to Pawtograder, they appear here automatically. Unknown capabilities appear under **Other** with their database description.
</Note>

## Staff & Roles

**Staff & Roles** has two tabs:

- **Staff** — lists every active grader with a dropdown to move them to a different role. Moving a grader takes effect immediately.
- **Roles** — lists every role with its active membership count and any removed members.

To make someone a grader or an instructor in the first place, use **Course Settings → Enrollments**. The Staff tab only moves existing graders between roles.

## Renaming and deleting custom roles

To rename or delete a custom role, go to **Create / Edit Role**, click **Edit a role**, select the role, then use the **Rename** or **Delete** button.

The default Grader role cannot be renamed or deleted.

A role with active or removed members cannot be deleted. The delete dialog tells you how many people are on the role and asks you to move them first. Removed graders count too — re-enable them under Enrollments, move them to another role, then delete.

## How roles appear in Enrollments

The **Enrollments** table shows each grader's staff role name in the Role column. If a grader is on a custom role, the column shows that role's name instead of "Grader". Changes made on the Staff Roles page appear in Enrollments without a reload, and vice versa.
