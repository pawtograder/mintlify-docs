# Staff Roles

Instructors can create custom grader roles that grant specific permissions beyond the default grader access. Custom roles let you give a TA or course assistant exactly the access they need — for example, the ability to manage surveys without giving them full instructor access.

Open **Course Settings → Staff Roles** (`manage/course/staff-roles`) to manage roles for your course.

## Default roles

Pawtograder has three built-in per-course roles:

| Role | What they can do |
|---|---|
| **Instructor** | Full management access to the course |
| **Grader** | Grade submissions, view analytics, and access a subset of Course Settings |
| **Student** | View course content, submit assignments, and respond to surveys and polls |

The grader role is the starting point for custom roles. A custom role inherits the grader's base access and adds the specific permissions you grant it.

## Custom roles

Custom roles are named roles you define for your course. Each custom role is assigned one or more **permissions** that unlock specific staff actions.

### Creating a custom role

1. Open **Course Settings → Staff Roles**
2. Click **Create Role**
3. Enter a name for the role (for example, "Survey Manager" or "Poll Runner")
4. Check the permissions you want to grant
5. Click **Save**

Once created, the role appears in the **Enrollments** page, where you can assign it to any grader in the course.

### Available permissions

| Permission | What it unlocks |
|---|---|
| `manage_surveys` | Create, edit, publish, close, and delete surveys; view all responses |
| `run_polls` | Create, open, close, and delete polls |
| `manage_flashcards` | Create and edit flashcard decks and cards; view all analytics |
| `send_announcements` | Compose and send email from **Course Settings → Emailer** |

<Note>
A grader without any custom role can still **view** surveys and polls (read-only access to published content and responses). The permissions above unlock the staff **action** controls — creating, editing, publishing, and deleting.
</Note>

### Assigning a custom role

1. Open **Course Settings → Enrollments**
2. Find the grader you want to assign the role to
3. Open their row and select the custom role from the **Role** dropdown
4. The change saves immediately

A grader can hold only one custom role at a time. Assigning a new role replaces the previous one.

### Editing and deleting roles

Click the pencil icon on a role row to edit its name or permissions. Changes take effect immediately for all graders currently assigned that role.

Click the trash icon to delete a role. Graders assigned the deleted role revert to the default grader role.

## What graders see by default

Without a custom role, a grader's Course Settings menu shows: **Discord**, **Lab Sections**, **Flashcard Decks**, **Discussion Engagement**, and **Grading Conflicts**. The following entries are instructor-only by default: **Enrollments**, **LMS (LTI) Sync**, **Feature flags**, **GitHub Templates**, **Discussion Topics**, **Due Date Extensions**, **Audit Log**, **Emailer**, and **Workflow Runs**.

Granting a custom role does not add new Course Settings entries to the grader's menu — it unlocks the action controls within the pages the grader can already reach, and adds the **Emailer** entry when `send_announcements` is granted.
